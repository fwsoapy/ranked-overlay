Add it to OBS

  1. Hit Copy OBS link up top.
  2. In OBS, click + under Sources and pick Browser. Name it whatever you like.
  3. Paste the link into URL. Set Width to 520 and Height to 240, then click OK.
  4. Drag it where you want it. That's it, it updates on its own.

To switch modes or flip between stats and your creator code, right-click the source in OBS and pick Interact. The buttons are just under the card, outside the area your viewers see.

Changed something here? Copy the link again and paste it over the old one (double-click the source in OBS to edit it).

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no download, no catch.

Do I need to run anything or turn off my antivirus?

No. It's a web page that OBS shows like any other browser source. Nothing runs on your PC.

Where do the numbers come from?

OliTracker. If something looks off, check your profile there first. OliTracker only publishes ELO for the top 10,000 of each mode, so the ELO row shows up once you're inside that and hides again if you drop out.

How often does it update?

About every 3 minutes while you're streaming or recording, right when OliTracker refreshes your profile, so you see new numbers as soon as they exist. When OBS is open but you're not live it checks every 10 minutes, and it stops completely while the overlay isn't on screen. It picks right back up when it is.

What does "+23 ELO TODAY" count from?

From when the overlay first loaded. Switching scenes doesn't reset it. Close OBS for more than an hour and it starts fresh next time.

It says "no ranked data found".

OliTracker has no ranked games for that account yet. Play a ranked match, or check you picked the right account.
