Add it to OBS

  1. Copy your link.
  2. In OBS, add a Browser source.
  3. Paste it into URL, keep Width at 800 and Height at 600 and click OK.
  4. Hold Alt and drag the bottom edge up to just under the card to crop out the buttons.

Right-click the source and pick Interact to switch modes, show your creator code or reset your ELO gain.

Record counts the games you play from your season totals, which OliTracker updates a few minutes after each game, so a win or loss can take a few minutes to show up.

Session starts over by itself after 3 hours with no ELO change, or when you press Reset ELO gain.

Changed something? Copy the link again and paste it over the old one.
